Privacy Policy

Darskee-Sales & Inventory App  ·  Android · Google Play

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Overview

Darskee Sales & Inventory is a POS and inventory management app for retail businesses, supporting sales tracking, barcode scanning, and offline profit reporting. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is treated.

Data We Collect

  • Product and inventory items you enter (names, prices, quantities, barcodes)
  • Sales transaction records you create
  • Business settings and preferences you configure

No account or login is required. No business data is transmitted to external servers.

How We Use It

  • Managing your product catalog and stock levels
  • Recording sales and generating profit/loss reports
  • Supporting barcode scanning for faster checkout

Data Storage

All business data is stored locally on your device and works fully offline. Nothing is uploaded to any server or cloud service. Your sales and inventory records never leave your device. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell, trade, or share your business data with any third parties.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics may be shared with Google via Google Play's built-in services, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

  • Access: All data is on your device and accessible within the app at any time.
  • Deletion: Delete individual records within the app, or clear all data via your device settings.
  • Contact: For any privacy concerns, email darilquervo.dev@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The latest version is always available at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
