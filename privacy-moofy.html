Privacy Policy

Moofy - Your Budget Buddy  ·  iOS · App Store

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Overview

Moofy is a personal budgeting app that helps you manage and track your money. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is treated.

Data We Collect

  • Budget entries and financial records you manually input (income, expenses, categories)
  • App settings and preferences you configure

No account or login is required. No financial data is transmitted to external servers.

How We Use It

  • Displaying your budget summary and spending breakdown
  • Persisting your records between app sessions

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server or cloud service. Your financial information never leaves your phone. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell, trade, or share your financial data with any third parties.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics may be shared with Apple via App Store's built-in services, subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Tracking

Moofy does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. No advertising identifiers are used.

Your Rights

  • Access: All your data is on your device and visible within the app.
  • Deletion: Delete individual records within the app, or delete the app to remove all data.
  • Contact: For any privacy concerns, email darilquervo.dev@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The latest version is always available at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
