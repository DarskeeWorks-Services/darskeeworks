Service Agreement

The standard terms for custom projects. Your approved written proposal sets the specific scope, price and timeline, and takes precedence where it differs.

Last updated: November 4, 2026 (based on the Terms of Service of July 13, 2026)

These terms govern web and mobile app development engagements between DarskeeWorks (Daril Quervo, "we", "us") and the client ("you"). Terms specific to an individual project are agreed in writing (email, messaging or your approved proposal) before work begins and take precedence over this general page where they differ.

1. Scope of services

Services covered by these terms include website development, mobile app development, business software, AI automation, UI/UX design and related consulting, as described on our Services page. The specific scope, deliverables and price for a project are agreed before work starts. Work outside that agreed scope is treated as a change request and may require an updated quote and timeline.

2. Payment terms

  • A deposit of 50% of the project total is due before work begins; the remaining balance is due on delivery, before final handoff of source files and store/account access.
  • For larger projects, payment may instead be split into milestones agreed in writing.
  • Prices on the Pricing and Services pages are starting points; the final price depends on project scope.
  • Late payment may pause work until the account is settled.
  • Third-party costs (domains, paid plugins, app store developer accounts, AI service usage, hosting) are paid by you, directly to the provider where possible.

3. Revisions

Each project includes the number of revision rounds stated in your package or proposal — 2 rounds if none is stated — within the agreed scope. Additional revision rounds, or changes outside the original scope, are billed separately or added to the timeline by agreement.

4. Timeline

Estimated delivery timelines are provided at the start of a project and depend on scope, complexity, and how quickly you provide feedback and required materials (content, assets, credentials). Delays on your side may shift the delivery date accordingly.

5. Intellectual property & ownership

Once a project is paid in full, ownership of the final deliverables (source code, designs and assets created specifically for you) transfers to you, excluding any third-party libraries, frameworks or licensed assets, which remain governed by their own licenses. Domains, store developer accounts and hosting accounts are registered in your name. DarskeeWorks may display the finished work in its portfolio unless you request otherwise in writing.

6. Support & maintenance

Bug fixes for issues present at delivery are covered for 30 days after handoff. Ongoing maintenance, new features or hosting after that period can be arranged separately — see Support & Maintenance.

7. No guaranteed outcomes

We follow good practices for performance, accessibility and search engines, but we do not guarantee specific results such as search rankings, traffic, sales, or approval by app stores, which depend on factors outside our control.

8. Liability

DarskeeWorks will make commercially reasonable efforts to deliver working, reliable software, but does not guarantee the service will be error-free or uninterrupted. To the extent permitted by law, DarskeeWorks' liability for any claim arising from a project is limited to the amount paid for that project. We are not liable for indirect or consequential damages, including lost profits or data loss, except where caused by our gross negligence or willful misconduct.

9. Cancellation

Either party may cancel a project in writing. If you cancel after work has started, the deposit and any completed milestone payments are non-refundable, covering work already performed; any additional work completed beyond that is billed at the agreed rate. See also the Refund Policy.

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws applicable to DarskeeWorks' place of business, without regard to conflict-of-law principles, unless a specific project agreement states otherwise.
