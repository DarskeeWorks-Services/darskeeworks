Service Agreement

The standard terms for custom projects. Your approved written proposal sets the specific scope, price and timeline, and takes precedence where it differs.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Proposal and scope

Each project starts with a written proposal that lists deliverables, timeline, revision rounds and price. Work begins once you approve the proposal and the down payment is received. Anything not listed in the proposal is outside the scope.

2. Payment

  • 50% down payment before work starts; 50% after your final approval and before launch or handover, unless the proposal states otherwise.
  • Accepted payment methods are listed in the proposal.
  • Third-party costs (domains, paid plugins, app store developer accounts, AI service usage, hosting) are paid by you, directly to the provider where possible.

3. Timeline and your responsibilities

Timelines assume timely feedback and materials from you. Please provide content (text, images, logos), account access and approvals when requested. Delays in feedback or materials move the delivery date accordingly.

4. Revisions and changes

A revision round is one consolidated list of changes to delivered work. Changes beyond the agreed rounds, or new features outside the scope, are quoted separately before any extra work is done.

5. Ownership

  • After full payment, you own the final deliverables created specifically for you.
  • Domains, store developer accounts and hosting accounts are registered in your name.
  • DarskeeWorks keeps ownership of its general tools, code libraries and know-how used to build your project, and grants you a perpetual license to use them as part of your project.
  • DarskeeWorks may show the finished project in its portfolio unless you ask us not to in writing.

6. Post-launch fixes

Each package includes a post-launch period (for example, 14 or 30 days) during which bugs in delivered work are fixed at no charge. New features and content changes are not included in that period, but can be covered by a maintenance plan.

7. No guaranteed outcomes

We build carefully and follow good practices for performance, accessibility and search engines. We do not guarantee specific results such as search rankings, traffic, sales, or approval by app stores, which depend on factors outside our control.

8. Confidentiality

We keep your non-public business information confidential and use it only for your project.

9. Cancellation

Either party may end a project in writing. You pay for work completed up to the cancellation date, as described in the Refund Policy, and receive the deliverables covered by what has been paid.

10. Liability

To the extent permitted by applicable law, DarskeeWorks' total liability for a project is limited to the fees paid for that project.
