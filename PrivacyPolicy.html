Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

Mochi Sensei ("we", "our", or "the app") is a Japanese language learning application developed by Daril Quervo. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use Mochi Sensei on your iPhone or iPad.

Information We Collect

Mochi Sensei is designed with privacy in mind. We collect only what is necessary to provide the app's core functionality:

  • Study data: Your flashcard progress, vocabulary learned, exam results, daily streaks, and XP — stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your personal iCloud account.
  • App preferences: Your selected JLPT level, notification settings, and study goals — stored locally.
  • API key (optional): If you choose to use the AI Sensei feature, you provide your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key. This key is stored securely on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA/IDFV for tracking).
  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or crash reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

iCloud Sync

If you are signed into iCloud, your study progress (flashcard states, XP, streak, exam history) is automatically synced via Apple's CloudKit service to your personal iCloud account. This data is governed by Apple's iCloud Privacy Policy. We cannot access your iCloud data.

AI Sensei Feature

The AI Sensei feature is entirely optional. To use it, you must supply your own API key from OpenAI or Anthropic. When you send a message to AI Sensei:

  • Your message is sent directly from your device to OpenAI or Anthropic using your API key.
  • We do not intercept, store, or log these conversations.
  • Please review OpenAI's Privacy Policy and Anthropic's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle your data.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Mochi Sensei declares microphone and speech recognition permissions for a future Japanese speech practice feature. These permissions are not actively used in the current version. If/when this feature is added, this policy will be updated.

Text-to-Speech

Japanese pronunciation audio is generated on-device using Apple's AVSpeechSynthesizer. No audio data leaves your device.

Push Notifications

With your permission, Mochi Sensei sends local daily study reminders. These are generated on-device and do not involve any server or third party.

Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device or in your personal iCloud account. You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling Mochi Sensei. iCloud data can be deleted from Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

Children's Privacy

Mochi Sensei is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains no inappropriate content and is suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
