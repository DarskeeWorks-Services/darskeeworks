Privacy Policy

How DarskeeWorks handles personal information on this website, in the digital shop, and in DarskeeWorks apps.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

On this page

  2. Website & inquiry form
  3. Digital shop purchases

This policy is provided by DarskeeWorks (Daril Quervo), Fukuoka, Japan. Each DarskeeWorks app also has its own privacy policy, linked in the app section. If an app's own policy differs from this page, the app's policy applies to that app.

General principles

  • We collect only the information needed to provide the service you ask for.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising profiles.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time.

Website & inquiry form

Information you give us

When you send a project inquiry, we receive the details you enter: name, email address, country, business type, the service you need, budget range, timeline and project description. When you email or message us on LINE, we receive what you send through those services.

How it is delivered

Inquiry form submissions are transmitted to us through a third-party form-processing service, or — if you choose — sent from your own email app. The form-processing service handles the data under its own privacy policy, solely to deliver your message to us.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and prepare a proposal.
  • To deliver and support a project you have agreed to.
  • We do not add you to a mailing list without your separate consent.

Cookies, analytics & third parties

This website does not use advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address under Google's Privacy Policy. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Retention

Inquiry emails are kept as long as needed to respond and, for clients, for the duration of the project and our record-keeping obligations. You may ask us to delete an inquiry at any time.

Digital shop purchases

Purchases are processed by a hosted checkout provider (such as Payhip or Gumroad). The provider collects your payment details directly — DarskeeWorks never receives or stores your card or bank details. We receive your name, email address and order details from the provider so we can deliver support and honor refund requests.

Software and apps listed in the shop that link to an app store or official product website are sold and delivered there, under that store's or site's terms and privacy policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws — such as the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173), Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information, or the GDPR — may give you rights to:

  • be informed about how your data is used;
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • request deletion of your data;
  • object to or withdraw consent for processing.

To make a request, email us using the contact details below.
