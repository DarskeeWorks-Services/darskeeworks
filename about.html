About DarskeeWorks

Build smarter. Work better. Grow digitally.

DarskeeWorks provides websites, mobile applications, business software, AI automation and ready-made digital products for individuals, educators, entrepreneurs and businesses.

Our mission

Practical technology that people actually use

Our mission is to make useful, reliable digital tools affordable for small businesses, teachers and independent professionals — without bloat, jargon or unnecessary monthly fees.

DarskeeWorks has two divisions:

  • Digital Studio — custom websites, mobile apps, business systems, AI automation and UI/UX design.
  • Digital Marketplace — ready-made planners, templates and software you can use right away.

Founder

Meet Daril Quervo

Daril is the founder and developer behind DarskeeWorks. He comes from a hands-on background as a Registered Master Electrician (Philippines) and a carpenter. While working in Japan as an Overseas Filipino Worker, he taught himself to build mobile apps and websites — and shipped real ones to the App Store and Google Play.

Because he has worked the trades, DarskeeWorks understands small, practical businesses. The goal is always the same: build things that are simple, reliable and actually used.

Languages: English · Japanese (N4) · Tagalog · Cebuano
