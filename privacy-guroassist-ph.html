Privacy Policy

GuroAssist PH - Your Virtual Assistant  ·  Website

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Overview

GuroAssist PH is a web-based virtual assistant service. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is treated.

Data We Collect

  • Messages and queries you submit through the assistant interface
  • Session data used to maintain your conversation context
  • Basic usage data (page visits, feature interactions) to improve the service

How We Use It

  • Processing your queries and generating assistant responses
  • Maintaining conversation context within a session
  • Improving assistant accuracy and service quality

Cookies & Local Storage

GuroAssist PH may use cookies or browser local storage to maintain your session and preferences. These are not used for advertising. You can clear them via your browser settings at any time.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • Queries may be processed by AI services to generate responses. These are handled under their respective privacy policies.
  • The app is hosted at guroassistph.site. Hosting infrastructure may collect standard server logs (IP address, timestamps) subject to the hosting provider's privacy policy.

Data Retention

Session data is cleared when you close or leave the app. We do not retain conversation history beyond the active session unless a history feature is explicitly enabled.

Your Rights

  • Deletion: Clear your browser cookies and session storage to remove locally stored session data.
  • Requests: To request deletion of any server-side data, email darilquervo.dev@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The latest version is always available at this URL. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
