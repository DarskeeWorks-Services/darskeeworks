Privacy Policy

Solar PV Calc - Solar Designer  ·  Android · Google Play

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Overview

Solar PV Calc is a solar panel design and calculation tool. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is treated.

Data We Collect

  • Input values you enter for calculations (system size, load consumption, location parameters, panel specs)
  • Saved calculation results stored locally on your device

No account or login is required. No data is transmitted to external servers.

How We Use It

  • Performing solar PV calculations and generating system design reports
  • Saving your inputs so you can continue a calculation session later

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server or cloud service. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics may be shared with Google via Google Play's built-in services, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

  • Access: All your data is on your device — you can view it anytime in the app.
  • Deletion: Clear app data via your device settings or uninstall the app.
  • Contact: For any privacy concerns, email darilquervo.dev@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The latest version is always available at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
