Privacy Policy

Darskee Automation  ·  Desktop Software

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Overview

Darskee Automation is a desktop software tool for AI-powered content creation — generating scripts, images, voiceovers, captions, and short-form videos for social media platforms. This policy explains what data the software handles and how it is treated.

Data We Collect

  • Content inputs you provide: niche topics, script prompts, and configuration settings
  • API credentials you enter for third-party AI services (e.g. Fal AI) — stored locally on your machine
  • Output files (images, audio, video) generated and saved to your local storage

No account registration is required to use Darskee Automation.

How We Use It

  • Processing your inputs to generate AI content via connected third-party APIs
  • Rendering and exporting completed videos to your local device

Third-Party AI Services

Darskee Automation connects to external AI APIs to generate content. Your prompts and inputs may be sent to these services as part of the generation process:

  • Fal AI — for AI image generation (subject to Fal AI's Privacy Policy)
  • Other AI voice and script generation services as configured

We do not control how these third-party services handle your data. Review their privacy policies before use.

Data Storage

All configuration data, API keys, and generated output files are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to DarskeeWorks servers. Uninstalling the software removes all locally stored application data.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties beyond the AI service APIs required for content generation.
  • Generated content (videos, images) is saved locally and shared only by your own actions.

Your Rights

  • Access: All data is stored on your device and accessible at any time.
  • Deletion: Delete the application and its data folder from your device to remove all stored data.
  • Contact: For any privacy concerns, email darilquervo.dev@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

This software is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the software evolves. The latest version is always available at this URL. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
