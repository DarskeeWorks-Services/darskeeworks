Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use Mochi Sensei?

No. Mochi Sensei works fully offline with no sign-in required. Your study progress is stored on your device and syncs automatically across your devices via iCloud if you're signed in.

How does the flashcard (SRS) system work?

Mochi Sensei uses a Spaced Repetition System. After each card, rate how well you knew it — Again, Hard, Good, or Easy — and the app schedules your next review at the right interval to help you retain it long-term.

What's included for free vs. Mochi Sensei Pro?

The core vocabulary, kanji, grammar library, flashcards, and mock exams are available for free. Mochi Sensei Pro (available as Monthly, Annual, or Lifetime) unlocks additional content and features. You can view current pricing and manage your plan from Settings inside the app.

How do I restore my purchase or cancel my subscription?

To restore a previous purchase, go to Settings inside the app and tap "Restore Purchases." Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID and can be viewed or cancelled anytime in iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

How do I use the AI Sensei chat feature?

AI Sensei is an optional feature. In Settings → AI Sensei, enter your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key to enable grammar help, sentence breakdowns, writing corrections, and conversation practice. If you don't have a key, every other feature in the app still works normally.

Is my study data private?

Yes. We don't collect analytics, track you across apps, or sell your data. Your progress stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

The app isn't loading my progress / streak reset unexpectedly.

Make sure iCloud is enabled for Mochi Sensei under iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → See All, and that you have an internet connection so sync can complete. If the issue continues, email us with your device model and iOS version.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

We'd love to hear it — email darilquervo.dev@gmail.com with a description of what happened and, if possible, steps to reproduce it.
