Yes. Mochi Sensei costs $1.99 once — no subscriptions, no recurring charges, no premium tier hidden behind a paywall. Every feature, including all future JLPT content updates, is included.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Mochi Sensei is fully offline-first — vocabulary, kanji, grammar, flashcards, and mock exams all work without a connection. iCloud sync and the optional AI Sensei chat require internet.

What is AI Sensei and do I need to pay extra for it?

AI Sensei is an optional chat tutor for grammar help, corrections, and conversation practice. It uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models first, and can fall back to your own OpenAI or Claude API key. No extra app purchase is required — you simply provide your own key if you want that fallback.

Which JLPT levels are covered?

All five: N5 through N1, with vocabulary, kanji, and grammar content for each level, plus timed mock exams that mirror the real test format.

What devices does it support?

Mochi Sensei runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18.0 or later, with study progress syncing automatically across your devices via iCloud.
