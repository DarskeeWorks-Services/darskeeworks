Digital Product License

What you can and can't do with DarskeeWorks planners, templates and other digital files.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

When you buy a digital product, DarskeeWorks grants you a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use it as described below. DarskeeWorks keeps ownership of the product. Each product page states which license type applies.

Personal use license

For one person — for example, a teacher using a planner for their own classes.

You may

  • print as many copies as you need for your own use;
  • use the files digitally on your own devices.

You may not

  • resell, share, or give away the files;
  • upload the files to shared drives, groups or marketplaces.

Single business use license

For one business or one end client.

You may

  • edit and customize the template for one business or one client project;
  • use the finished, customized designs commercially (for example, publish your social media posts or send your invoices).

You may not

  • resell or share the editable template itself;
  • use it for more than one business without buying another license.

Single website license

For website templates.

You may

  • build and launch one website, for yourself or for one client;
  • modify the code and design for that website.

You may not

  • resell or redistribute the template, modified or not;
  • use it for multiple websites without buying additional licenses.

All licenses

  • You may not include the products, or parts of them, in other products for sale (such as template packs or courses).
  • Third-party elements, such as fonts or Canva library elements, remain subject to their own licenses.
  • The license ends automatically if you breach these terms.
  • Need a school-wide, multi-user or extended commercial license? Contact us.

Software and apps

Apps and software in the shop are licensed under the terms shown in their app store or on their official website.
