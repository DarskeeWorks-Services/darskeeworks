Privacy Policy

DarsKaraoke - Sing Your Heart Out  ·  Website

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Overview

DarsKaraoke is a web app for singing karaoke songs with friends. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is treated.

Data We Collect

  • Song search queries you enter during a session
  • Session data used to maintain your current karaoke session (stored in browser cookies or local storage)

No account or registration is required to use DarsKaraoke.

How We Use It

  • Serving karaoke content based on your song searches
  • Maintaining your active session while you use the app

Cookies & Local Storage

DarsKaraoke may use browser cookies or local storage to maintain your session. These are not used for tracking or advertising. You can clear them via your browser settings at any time.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties.
  • The app is hosted on Railway. Usage may be subject to Railway's Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

  • Deletion: Clear your browser cookies and local storage to remove any session data.
  • Contact: For any privacy concerns, email darilquervo.dev@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The latest version is always available at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
