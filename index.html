About

A builder, on-site and on-screen.

I'm Daril, founder of DarskeeWorks — a small app & web studio based in Japan. I come from a hands-on background as a licensed Master Electrician and carpenter. Working in Japan as an Overseas Filipino Worker, I taught myself to build mobile apps and websites, and shipped real ones to the App Store and Google Play.

Because I've run the tools and worked the trades, DarskeeWorks understands small, practical businesses. I build things that are simple, reliable, and actually used — no bloat, no jargon. If you need a website or app that just works and looks professional, I can help.
