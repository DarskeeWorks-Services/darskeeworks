Refund & Delivery Policy

How delivery works and when refunds are available for digital products, services and maintenance plans.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Digital product delivery

  • Digital products are delivered by our hosted checkout provider. After the provider confirms your payment, it shows a download link and/or emails it to the address you used at checkout.
  • If you don't receive your download, check your spam folder, then email us with your order number.
  • Some products (for example Canva or Google Sheets templates) are delivered as a PDF with links that create your own copy.

Digital product refunds

Because digital files can be downloaded immediately, purchases are generally non-refundable once the file has been delivered. We will refund your purchase if, within 14 days of purchase:

  • the file is defective or cannot be opened, and we are unable to fix or replace it;
  • the product is materially different from its description; or
  • you were charged twice for the same product by mistake.

To request a refund, email darilquervo.dev@gmail.com with your order number and the reason. Approved refunds are issued through the checkout provider to your original payment method. The time it takes to appear depends on the provider and your bank.

Apps and externally sold software

Apps from the App Store or Google Play are refunded by Apple or Google under their own policies. Software sold on its own official website follows the refund terms shown there.

Custom services

  • Before work starts: if you cancel after paying the down payment but before work begins, the down payment is refunded, minus any third-party costs already paid on your behalf.
  • After work starts: payments cover work already completed. Any refund for work not yet performed is handled as set out in your approved proposal.
  • Revision rounds and post-launch fixes are provided as described in your package or proposal.

Maintenance plans

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time before the next billing cycle. Partial months are not refunded, and unused hours do not roll over.

Your statutory rights

This policy does not limit any rights you have under consumer protection laws that cannot be excluded.
